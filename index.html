Culture & Heritage

🇱🇰 A Rich Cultural Heritage

Sri Lanka has a long history and a diverse cultural heritage influenced by different communities and traditions.

🏛️ Ancient Heritage

Ancient cities such as Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa, together with places such as Sigiriya and Dambulla, are important parts of Sri Lanka's heritage.

🛕 Religious Heritage

Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic and Christian traditions contribute to Sri Lanka's diverse cultural landscape.

🎉 Festivals

Sri Lanka celebrates many cultural and religious festivals throughout the year.

💃 Traditional Arts

Kandyan dancing, traditional drumming, handicrafts and other artistic traditions are important parts of Sri Lankan culture.

Weather Guide ☀️

🌴 Sri Lanka's Tropical Climate

Weather can vary between different regions of the island, so check the local forecast before travelling.

🌧️ Southwest Monsoon

Generally affects the southwest areas during May–September.

🌦️ Second Inter-monsoon

October–November can bring changing weather across different parts of the island.

☀️ Northeast Monsoon

Generally affects northeastern areas during December–February.
